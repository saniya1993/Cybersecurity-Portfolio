# Activity 01 — Professional Statement

**Certificate:** Google Cybersecurity Professional Certificate  
**Activity:** Draft a Professional Statement 
**Status:** Completed

## Activity Overview

The purpose of this activity was to develop a professional statement introducing my background, strengths, values, interests, and career objectives in cybersecurity.

## Learning Objectives

- Identify professional strengths relevant to cybersecurity.
- Reflect on ethical values and professional responsibilities.
- Explain my interest in cybersecurity.
- Describe how my expertise can support organisational security goals.
- Communicate my professional identity clearly and effectively.

## Reflection

This activity helped me articulate how my interdisciplinary research background in cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, wireless communications, and federated learning contributes to my professional development in cybersecurity.

It also reinforced the importance of ethical responsibility, continuous learning, and applying technical expertise to protect information systems and support organisational security.
