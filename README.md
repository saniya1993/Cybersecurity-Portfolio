## About Me

I am a Research Fellow in Cybersecurity at the University of the West of England (UWE Bristol), UK, holding a PhD in Electrical Engineering with interdisciplinary research expertise in Artificial Intelligence, Wireless Communications, Federated Learning, and Internet of Things (IoT).

My current research focuses on cybersecurity, adversarial machine learning, federated learning security, and developing trustworthy, privacy-preserving, and resilient intelligent systems.

This repository documents my cybersecurity research, professional training, practical exercises, and technical projects.

## Professional Statement

My professional values include ethical responsibility, research excellence, analytical problem-solving, innovation, and continuous learning. I aim to contribute to the development of secure and trustworthy technologies that protect sensitive information and strengthen organisational cybersecurity.

## AI Security Research

My cybersecurity research interests include evaluating adversarial vulnerabilities in machine learning and federated learning systems, with a particular focus on robustness, privacy, and security.

Current research directions include:

- Adversarial example attacks, including FGSM, PGD, and C&W
- Adversarial defence mechanisms
- Federated learning robustness
- Security of AI-based medical image classification
- Trustworthy and resilient machine learning
