## Programme Overview

This folder documents my participation in the CCS05 Cybersecurity Training Programme, combining instructor-led learning with guided practical exercises across key cybersecurity disciplines.

As a Research Fellow in Cybersecurity at UWE Bristol, I am undertaking this training to strengthen my hands-on cybersecurity skills and complement my academic research in AI security, federated learning, and trustworthy intelligent systems.

## Learning Objectives

- Understand cybersecurity roles and professional pathways.
- Investigate phishing attacks and suspicious messages.
- Explore network traffic, controls, and security weaknesses.
- Understand authorised ethical hacking.
- Learn security monitoring, threat triage, and SOC operations.
- Explore governance, risk management, and compliance.
- Understand digital forensic evidence and incident response.

## Practical Learning Documentation

For each completed practical activity, I aim to document:

1. Activity objectives
2. Tools and technologies used
3. Methodology
4. Observations and results
5. Screenshots or evidence, where permitted
6. Key learning outcomes
7. Personal reflections
