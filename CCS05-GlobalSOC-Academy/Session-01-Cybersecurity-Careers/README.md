## 1. Session Overview

The first session of the CCS05 Cybersecurity Training Programme introduced cybersecurity career pathways, the purpose of the training initiative, and the importance of structured professional development.

The session included an introduction to Cyber Circle, an overview of GlobalSOC Academy and the training programme, a preview of the curriculum, and a discussion of career opportunities within cybersecurity.

A key message was that cybersecurity is a broad professional field encompassing multiple specialisations beyond the commonly recognised roles.

The session also highlighted the challenges associated with entering cybersecurity and the importance of developing appropriate technical skills, receiving professional guidance, and selecting a career pathway aligned with individual interests and capabilities.

## 2. Session Objectives

- Understand the breadth of career opportunities within cybersecurity.
- Recognise the challenges associated with entering the cybersecurity profession.
- Explore the importance of developing relevant technical skills and professional competencies.
- Understand the role of structured training and professional guidance in cybersecurity career development.
- Gain an overview of the CCS05 training programme and its learning approach.

## 3. Session Notes

### 3.1 Introduction to Cyber Circle and the Programme

The session introduced Cyber Circle and explained the purpose of the cybersecurity training initiative.

The programme aims to provide participants with exposure to different cybersecurity disciplines and support their professional development through structured learning.

### 3.2 GlobalSOC Academy and Training Approach

GlobalSOC Academy introduced its training programme and discussed an approach based on three interconnected elements:

**Exposure, Experience, and Education**

- **Exposure:** Gaining awareness of cybersecurity domains, professional roles, and industry practices.
- **Experience:** Developing practical understanding through relevant learning activities and hands-on opportunities.
- **Education:** Building foundational knowledge and technical competencies through structured training.

This framework was presented as a way to support participants in developing relevant skills and identifying suitable cybersecurity career pathways.

### 3.3 Cybersecurity Career Pathways

The session emphasised that cybersecurity is a broad field offering multiple career opportunities.

Roles and domains explicitly mentioned in the session included:

- Security Operations Centre (SOC) Analyst
- Incident Response
- Digital Forensics and Incident Response (DFIR)

The speaker also highlighted that many additional cybersecurity roles and specialisations exist beyond those commonly recognised.

This reinforced the importance of exploring different cybersecurity domains before selecting a particular career direction.

### 3.4 Challenges in Entering Cybersecurity

The speaker acknowledged that obtaining a first cybersecurity role can be challenging.

The discussion emphasised the importance of:

- Developing relevant technical skills.
- Receiving appropriate professional support and guidance.
- Understanding the different cybersecurity domains.
- Selecting a career pathway based on individual strengths and interests.
- Following a structured approach to professional development.

### 3.5 Programme Curriculum Overview

The CCS05 programme introduces participants to several cybersecurity disciplines through seven instructor-led sessions:

1. Cybersecurity Careers and Professional Pathways
2. Cyberattacks and Phishing Simulation
3. Network Security
4. Offensive Security and Ethical Hacking
5. Defensive Security and SOC Operations
6. Governance, Risk and Compliance (GRC)
7. Digital Forensics and Incident Response (DFIR)

These topics provide opportunities to explore both technical and non-technical aspects of cybersecurity.

## 4. Key Learning Outcomes

The main learning points I identified from the session were:

**1. Cybersecurity offers diverse professional pathways.**

Cybersecurity extends beyond a single technical role and includes multiple areas of specialisation, such as security operations, incident response, and digital forensics.

**2. Career development requires more than theoretical knowledge.**

A combination of education, practical experience, and exposure to professional cybersecurity environments can support career development.

**3. Choosing an appropriate specialisation is important.**

Understanding the different cybersecurity domains can help professionals identify career pathways that align with their existing expertise, interests, and future goals.

**4. Practical skills and professional guidance are valuable.**

Developing technical competencies and learning from experienced professionals can help individuals navigate challenges when entering or transitioning within cybersecurity.

**5. Continuous professional development is essential.**

Structured training and ongoing learning can help professionals broaden their knowledge and adapt to evolving cybersecurity requirements.
